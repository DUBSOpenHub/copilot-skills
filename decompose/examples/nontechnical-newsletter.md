```markdown
# Launch a Weekly Sustainable Living Newsletter

**For:** Creator / Writer · **Created:** 2024-01-15 · **Session:** sess-nontechnical-newsletter-001

---

## Goal
Launch a weekly email newsletter about sustainable living, growing to 100 subscribers over 3 months.

## Why This Matters
You want to share ideas about sustainable living with people who care about the same things you do. A newsletter lets you build a loyal audience who chose to hear from you — on a schedule you control, without relying on any social media algorithm.

## What We Know
- Weekly sending cadence
- Topic: sustainable living
- Starting audience: friends, family, and Instagram followers (~200 reachable contacts)
- 100-subscriber goal within 3 months
- You'll write all the content yourself
- Substack is a strong platform option for first-time newsletter writers

## What We Don't Know Yet
- Which email platform you'll use (this is the first choice to make)

## The Plan
1. **Choose your email platform** — Pick a tool that fits how you write. Substack is free, easy, and built for writers. Sign up takes about 10 minutes.
2. **Set up your newsletter** — Give it a name, add a short description, and write a welcome note for new subscribers.
3. **Invite your first readers** — Text or email your friends. Share your sign-up link on Instagram. Aim for 30+ subscribers before your first send.
4. **Write and prepare issue #1** — Write your first piece. Keep it personal and honest about why you're starting this.
5. **Approve and send issue #1** — You review it, feel good about it, and press send.
6. **Repeat weekly and track growth** — Send every week. Watch your subscriber count. Celebrate hitting 100.

## Decisions You Need to Make
1. **Choose email platform** — Pick before anything else. All other steps depend on this.
2. **Send first email** — Sending to subscribers is public and can't be unsent. You'll review and approve it yourself.

## Risks and Watch-Outs
- If you don't build your subscriber list before issue #1, it may go to very few readers
- I assumed you're writing solo — let me know if that changes

## How to Know It Worked
- Your newsletter is live and accepting sign-ups
- 100 or more people have subscribed within 3 months
- You've published at least 12 issues consistently

## Next Step
To begin, choose your email platform — Substack is the easiest starting point for new newsletter writers.
```
