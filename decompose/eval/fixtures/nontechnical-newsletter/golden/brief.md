# Launch a Weekly Sustainable Living Newsletter

**For:** Creator / Writer · **Created:** 2024-01-15T10:00:00Z · **Session:** sess-nontechnical-newsletter-golden

---

## Goal

Launch a weekly email newsletter about sustainable living, growing to 100 subscribers over 3 months.

---

## Why This Matters

You want to share ideas about sustainable living with people who care about the same things. A newsletter lets you build a loyal audience who chose to hear from you — on a schedule you control, without depending on any social media platform.

---

## What We Know

- Weekly sending schedule
- Topic: sustainable living
- Starting audience of friends, family, and Instagram followers (roughly 200 reachable contacts)
- Goal: 100 subscribers within 3 months
- You will write all the content yourself
- Substack is a strong option for first-time newsletter writers

---

## What We Don't Know Yet

- Which email platform you will use (this is the first choice to make — everything else depends on it)

---

## The Plan

1. **Choose your email platform** — Pick a tool you are comfortable with. Substack is free and easy for writers. Sign-up takes about 10 minutes.
2. **Set up your newsletter** — Give it a name, write a short description, and set up a welcome message for new readers.
3. **Invite your first readers** — Text or email friends. Share your sign-up link on Instagram. Aim for at least 30 readers before your first send.
4. **Write issue #1** — Write your first piece. Keep it personal and explain why you are starting this newsletter.
5. **Review and send issue #1** — You read it over, feel good about it, and press send yourself.
6. **Send every week and track growth** — Publish on a regular day each week. Celebrate when you reach 100 readers.

---

## Decisions You Need to Make

1. **Choose your email platform** — Pick this first. All other steps depend on your choice.
2. **Send your first email** — Sending to real readers is public and cannot be unsent. You will review it and approve it yourself before it goes out.

---

## Risks and Watch-Outs

- If you do not have readers ready before issue #1, your first send may go to very few people
- I assumed you are writing this newsletter on your own — let me know if that changes and I will update the plan

---

## How to Know It Worked

- Your newsletter is live and people can sign up
- 100 or more people have subscribed within 3 months
- You have published at least 12 issues in a row without missing a week

---

## Next Step

To begin, choose your email platform — Substack is the easiest starting point for a first-time newsletter writer.
